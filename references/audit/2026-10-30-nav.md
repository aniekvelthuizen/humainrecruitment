**Broken**
1. **Mobile menu after scrolling** — scroll down, open the menu: Contact and Careers are gone, an empty gap above the button. *Fix: limit the top-row fold rule in the nav CSS to the desktop bar.*
2. **Desktop dropdowns** — hover opens For business / About us, a click on the same item then closes it (2nd click reopens). *Fix: ignore mouse clicks on desktop toggles while hover has them open (Enter/Space stay).*
3. **Keyboard focus in a dropdown card** — at the top of a dark page the focus ring is white on the white card, and it clips the tops of the letters. *Fix: give the card its own dark text theme instead of a fixed colour (also fixes the Lumos item below).*

**Off**
4. **Keyboard leaves an open menu** — mobile: Tab goes to page content behind the white panel and Escape then does nothing; desktop: the card stays open after tabbing past its last link. *Fix: close the menu/card when focus leaves it (nav script).*
5. **Mobile keyboard order** — the button (shown last) gets focus before Contact and Careers. *Fix: put the Contact/Careers list before the button in the Menu.*
6. **Hamburger tap area** — 32 × 16 px, below the 44 × 44 minimum. *Fix: invisible padding on the button.*
7. **Links inside an open mobile row** — 21 px tall with 19 px dead space between them. *Fix: padding on the links instead of gap, same 40 px rhythm.*
8. **Mobile panel top** — the first row sits ~20 px closer under the bar than in the design (117 vs 137). *Fix: 1.25rem extra top padding on the panel.*
9. **Desktop dropdown cards** — links ~7 px closer together than the design (40 vs ~47; label → link 29 vs ~36), cards 28–60 px shorter. *Fix: match the design's item rhythm.*
10. **Closing the mobile menu on a dark page** — logo and hamburger fade to white while the white panel is still there and almost vanish. *Fix: keep the dark bar colours until the 300 ms close is done.*
11. **Focus ring on bar links and mobile rows** — drawn on the letters (inner offset), touches the "E" of Expertise. *Fix: positive outline offset in the nav.*
12. **Dropdown chevrons** — thinner and flatter than the design (~1 px vs ~2 px stroke). *Fix: use the design's chevron from the Icon Set (Designer).*

**Tidy**
13. **Reduced motion** — the mobile panel still wipes open in 300 ms. *Fix: also shorten the animation in the reduced-motion rule.*
14. **Scrolled bar colour** — white 80 % is a literal colour. *Fix: derive it from the nav background variable.*
15. **Leftover Lumos code** — nav banner rules + its sessionStorage script, "open on mobile" rules; nothing on the site uses them. *Fix: remove from the Nav's Lumos embeds.*
16. **Extra landmarks** — each dropdown card is an unlabelled `<nav>` inside the main nav (8 in total). *Fix: make them divs.*
17. **Records** — Menu is in component group "Miscellaneous" (nav.md says Global); nav.md gives two different hamburger gaps (0.875 and 0.75rem; built 12 px). *Fix: move Menu to Global, correct nav.md.*
18. **Skip link on Example Components** — points to #main, which that page lacks. *Fix: id `main` on its main wrapper.*

**Your call**
❓ **Q1 - Home has no nav**: the logo links to `/`, which is still the Lumos style-guide splash without the nav (and a stray Opportunity card under `<body>` makes it scroll sideways). ➡️ Put the Nav on Home now and remove the stray card.
❓ **Q2 - Expertise card column gap**: Sectors sit 48 px from the roles, design ~64. ➡️ Make it 64 (4rem literal, noted in decisions).
❓ **Q3 - "For candidates" and the button 4 px left / button 4 px wider than the design**: comes from Button Main padding. ➡️ Accept and record it in button.md.
❓ **Q4 - Nav code finds parts by class name**, not by `data-` attributes (kit rule). ➡️ Record it as an exception in nav.md; the classes are Lumos' own and stable.
❓ **Q5 - Current page in the nav**: the current link is marked in the code but looks the same; the design is silent. ➡️ Leave as is.
