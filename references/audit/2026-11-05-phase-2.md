### Does the hidden form cause anything?
Nothing a visitor or the bot check would notice.
- **Turnstile:** it renders only for the visible form, with one `cf-turnstile-response` per page. The hidden form's observer never fires and its submit stays disabled.
- **Blocking:** `required` on hidden fields cannot block the visible form, because it is a separate form.
- **Markup:** no duplicate ids.
- **Map:** not requested on `/submit-resume`.
- **Leftovers:** 6.6 kB of hidden form on `/contact` and 3.5 kB on `/submit-resume`. Crawlers can read the hidden text: "Why join the pool?" (H3) on `/contact`, the contact details on `/submit-resume`.
