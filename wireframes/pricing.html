FAQ

Pricing questions, answered

How can your fees be 25–30% lower?

AI handles the repetitive sourcing and benchmarking, so we spend less time per placement and pass that efficiency on to you.

Is there a replacement guarantee?

Yes — permanent placements include a replacement guarantee. The exact terms are set out in your agreement.

Do you charge upfront?

No upfront fee for standard search. Scoped consulting engagements are agreed and priced before we start.

How is secondment pricing structured?

A transparent hourly margin on top of the specialist's rate — you always see what you pay for.

Can you work to a fixed project price?

Yes. For project-basis engagements we agree a fixed price against a defined scope and milestones.
