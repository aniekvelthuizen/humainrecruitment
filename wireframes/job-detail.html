About the role

As Project Controls Manager you set up and run the controls framework — cost, planning, risk and reporting — on a multi-year capital project in the energy sector.

Responsibilities

  • Own cost control, forecasting and variance analysis
  • Maintain the integrated schedule and critical-path analysis
  • Run risk and performance reviews with the leadership team
  • Deliver clear, executive-level reporting and dashboards

Requirements

  • 12+ years in project controls on capital projects
  • Strong cost & planning background (P6 / EVM)
  • Oil & gas or energy sector experience
  • Fluent Dutch and English

What we offer

  • A senior role on a high-value international programme
  • Transparent terms and ongoing account support
  • Long-term delivery with a strong client
