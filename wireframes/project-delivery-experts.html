The bar

What "pre-qualified" means here.

A pre-qualified expert isn't just available — they're evidenced. We assess delivery track record, technical depth and fit before they enter the pool, and we keep that assessment current.

  • Evidenced delivery, not self-reported claims
  • Validated technical depth in their discipline
  • Benchmarked against the live market
  • Continuously re-assessed, not a one-off check
Our assessment process

Six steps, every expert

What happens on our side before a name reaches your shortlist.

Step 1

Source

AI scans the market and our network for relevant specialists in the discipline.

Step 2

Recruiter screening

An experienced recruiter reviews the profile, the story and the gaps.

Step 3

Technical validation

We test real competency and delivery depth — not keyword matches.

Step 4

Market benchmarking

The profile is benchmarked against comparable experts on real signals.

Step 5

Reference & track record

We verify delivery history and references on past programmes.

Step 6

Scored & pooled

An explainable match score is set and the expert enters the pre-qualified pool.
