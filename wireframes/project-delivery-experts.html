Our assessment process

Nine stages, every expert

What happens on our side before a name reaches your shortlist — the HumAIn Expert Qualification Framework.

📝 Accept / reject
Stage 1

Application & CV Submission

Basic eligibility check. Candidate submits CV, LinkedIn profile, contact details, location & availability and desired contract type.

Assessed
  • Project delivery background
  • Relevant discipline
  • Career consistency
  • Language requirements
  • Industry relevance
🔍 Top 30%
Stage 2

Profile Screening

Validate career quality and relevance. A recruiter and AI assessment system review career progression, project size, leadership exposure, industry expertise and longevity in roles.

Example checks
  • Worked on €100M+ projects?
  • Responsible for budgets?
  • Managed teams?
  • Relevant sectors?
🕵️ Top 20%
Stage 3

Experience Validation

Verify claimed achievements. Instead of accepting CV statements, HumAIn investigates actual responsibilities, reporting lines, project value, team size and stakeholder complexity.

We investigate
  • Was candidate lead planner?
  • Team size & duration
  • Reporting lines
  • Outcome achieved
🧪 Scored
Stage 4

Technical Assessment

Assess professional competence with discipline-specific testing — Project Controls, Planning & Scheduling, Cost Control and Project Management.

Examples by discipline
  • Earned Value Management & forecasting
  • Primavera P6 & critical path analysis
  • Cost engineering & variance analysis
  • Governance, risk & stakeholder mgmt
🧭 Scored
Stage 5

Behaviour & Performance Assessment

Predict real-world delivery success. Candidates describe a project recovery, a major conflict resolution and a significant project risk.

Assessed
  • Communication & leadership
  • Decision making & ownership
  • Resilience
  • Stakeholder management
☎️ Verified
Stage 6

Reference Validation

Independent verification via references from project directors, functional managers, clients and programme leaders.

Questions asked
  • Would you hire them again?
  • Biggest strengths?
  • Improvement areas?
  • Level compared to peers?
👥 Approved
Stage 7

Expert Review Panel

Human validation. Senior specialists review the CV, assessment results, references and market positioning.

They determine
  • Seniority level
  • Sector fit
  • Deployment readiness
📊 Ranked
Stage 8

Benchmarking & Ranking

Compare professionals against peers across 200+ industry skills — technical capability, leadership, delivery experience and reference strength.

Example score
  • Technical — 92
  • Leadership — 87
  • Delivery — 95
  • References — 90
✅ Certified
Stage 9

Accepted into HumAIn Network

Only candidates that surpass the benchmark enter the pool, with status: Certified HumAIn Expert.

Profile includes
  • Qualification score
  • Sector & role specialization
  • Availability & mobility
  • Language capability
What we assess

The criteria behind the score

Every professional in the HumAIn network is assessed against a consistent framework that combines experience, capability, commercial impact and verified performance.

📈 1 of 8
Criteria 1

Delivery Evidence

Proof of outcomes on real projects. We look beyond responsibilities and focus on measurable contribution — candidates must demonstrate successful delivery on complex projects, programmes or portfolios with clear evidence of impact.

We assess
  • Project value and complexity
  • Delivery milestones achieved
  • Programme outcomes
  • Track record of execution
🧪 2 of 8
Criteria 2

Technical Depth

Demonstrated expertise within the discipline. Technical knowledge is validated against real-world application, not simply certifications or years of experience.

We assess
  • Cost Management
  • Planning & Scheduling
  • Project Controls
  • Risk & Performance Management
🏗️ 3 of 8
Criteria 3

Sector Experience

Relevant industry exposure. Specialist industries require specialist knowledge — we assess whether experience aligns with the sectors our clients operate in.

We assess
  • Oil & Gas
  • Energy & Renewables
  • Infrastructure
  • Industrial & Manufacturing
💼 4 of 8
Criteria 4

Commercial Exposure

Understanding business impact. Professionals are evaluated on their ability to balance delivery performance with commercial objectives and stakeholder expectations.

We assess
  • Budget ownership
  • Contract management
  • Commercial decision-making
  • Executive stakeholder exposure
🗣️ 5 of 8
Criteria 5

Leadership & Soft Skills

The ability to influence and deliver through people. Technical expertise alone is not enough — we assess communication, leadership, collaboration and decision-making capability.

We assess
  • Stakeholder management
  • Communication effectiveness
  • Leadership capability
  • Problem solving
📅 6 of 8
Criteria 6

Availability & Mobility

Deployment readiness. The best professional is only valuable if they can realistically support a programme when needed.

We assess
  • Availability date
  • Contract preference
  • Travel flexibility
  • Geographic mobility
🌐 7 of 8
Criteria 7

Language Capability

Effective communication in project environments. We verify working proficiency rather than assumed fluency.

We assess
  • English proficiency
  • Local language requirements
  • Client-facing communication
  • International project readiness
☎️ 8 of 8
Criteria 8

References & Validation

Independent verification of performance. Every professional is benchmarked against feedback from previous managers, clients and project stakeholders.

We assess
  • Reference quality
  • Rehire likelihood
  • Peer comparison
  • Consistency of feedback
The weighting

How the final score is calculated

Not every criterion counts equally. Delivery evidence and technical depth carry the most weight — availability and language are qualifying, not decisive.

  • Delivery Evidence 25%
  • Technical Depth 20%
  • Sector Experience 15%
  • Leadership & Soft Skills 15%
  • Commercial Exposure 10%
  • References 10%
  • Availability 5%
